# 🌕 Moonscapes in Motion
### *Tracking Crater Evolution Across Lunar Seas*
 
> An interactive, scroll-driven data story visualizing the formation and long-term evolution of impact craters across the Moon's mare regions — from first impact to billion-year degradation.

[View Our Website](https://be910.github.io/LunarCrater/)
---
 
## Overview
 
**Moonscapes in Motion** is a narrative data visualization project that brings lunar geology to life through scrollytelling and interactive rendering. As you scroll through the site, the story of a crater unfolds — from the violent milliseconds of impact, to the slow sculpting of ejecta blankets, to the gradual infilling by ancient lava flows across the lunar *maria*.
 
---
 
## ✨ Features

### 🌑 Animated Moon Surface Rendering
The heart of the project is a **D3-rendered lunar globe** that animates crater evolution from the beginning of the Moon's history to today. At each timestep, craters appear, grow, and interact with one another — visually capturing the transition from a heavily bombarded early surface to the comparatively quieter modern Moon.

### 📜 Scroll-Driven Storytelling
Narrative sections advance in sync with the user's scroll position — text annotations fade in alongside their corresponding visual states, guiding the reader through deep geological time one chapter at a time.
 
### 🗺️ Mare Region Explorer & Analysis
Interactive map of the Moon's near side highlighting major *maria* — click any region to explore its crater population with age estimates and morphological classifications.

## 📖 The Story We Tell
 
> *Four billion years ago, the inner solar system was a shooting gallery. The Moon, lacking any atmosphere or plate tectonics to erase the evidence, kept every scar. Each crater is a timestamp — a frozen record of a collision that happened before the first complex life stirred on Earth.*
>
> *Moonscapes in Motion lets you scroll through that record: watching a crater born, watching it age, and watching the dark volcanic seas slowly rise to swallow it whole.*
